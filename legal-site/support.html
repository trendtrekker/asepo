Common questions

How does free access work?

Guests and signed-in users can use Asepo for 3 days with up to 3 recipe imports per UTC calendar day. Closing the paywall with X opens Home without starting a paid subscription. The app shows your remaining allowance.

What does Continue Free mean?

On the eligible monthly subscription offer, Continue Free starts Apple's purchase confirmation for a 3-day introductory trial. After the trial, the monthly subscription renews at the price shown unless cancelled. Confirm the offer and billing details on Apple's purchase sheet. This is separate from using X to continue without subscribing.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone or iPad, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions and select Asepo. You can manage or cancel the subscription there. Deleting Asepo or your Asepo account does not cancel an App Store subscription.

Open Apple subscription management

I paid, but Asepo Pro is not available

Sign in to your Asepo account, open Profile > Asepo Pro, then choose Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that originally purchased the subscription. If access is still missing, email support with the error message and purchase date.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles refunds for App Store purchases. You can request one through Apple's Report a Problem page. Approval is determined by Apple.

A recipe import failed

Check your connection and remaining import allowance. For a link, check that the recipe is publicly accessible. You can also try pasted recipe text or a clear photo. If it still fails, contact us with the source link or error message and the import method you used.

I cannot sign in

Use the same sign-in method you used to create your account: email, Apple or Google. If an email confirmation is requested, check your inbox and spam folder. Contact support if you still cannot access your account.

How do I delete my account or request help with my data?

Sign in and open Profile > Delete account. For access, correction, export or deletion requests, email support from your account email address. See our Privacy Policy for more information.
